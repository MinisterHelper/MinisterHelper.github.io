Dmoney — foreground location review

Android app version 1.0 (1). A short review recording of the trip-start, foreground notification, Home, and trip-finish lifecycle.

Recording walkthrough

  1. Start and permissions: start a trip and respond to the requested Android location and notification permissions.
  2. Foreground notification: inspect the persistent trip notification while a trip is active.
  3. Home and return: go to the Android Home screen, then return to Dmoney while the trip is active.
  4. Finish: finish the trip and confirm the ongoing trip notification disappears.

This recording uses a stationary Android emulator and a synthetic trip. It illustrates the app and notification lifecycle; it is not a real-road test or evidence of real-world location or distance accuracy.
